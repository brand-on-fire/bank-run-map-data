# Notice

This repository republishes and transforms public data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FDIC, FFIEC, Federal Reserve, U.S. Treasury or the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Data are provided as is; check the originals before relying on them.

- FDIC BankFind Suite API (api.fdic.gov): U.S. government work.
- FFIEC Central Data Repository (cdr.ffiec.gov): public call report data.
- U.S. Department of the Treasury, daily par yield curve rates (home.treasury.gov): U.S. government work.
- Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, H.15 Selected Interest Rates, retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (fred.stlouisfed.org).
- Federal Reserve discount window collateral margins (frbdiscountwindow.org).
- Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Federal Reserve District Boundaries (1996), via FRASER.
- U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 county list.
- Federal Reserve Bank of New York, CRSP-FRB link (Sept 30, 2024). The NY Fed makes no guarantee about its accuracy.
